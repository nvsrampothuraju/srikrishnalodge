Frequently Asked Questions — Sri Krishna Lodge Antarvedi

What is the best lodge near Antarvedi Temple?

Sri Krishna Lodge is the best-rated and most affordable lodge near Antarvedi Laxmi Narasimha Swamy Temple. We offer clean AC rooms, Non-AC rooms and suite rooms with attached bathrooms, free parking, and warm hospitality.

How to book a room in Antarvedi?

You can book a room at Sri Krishna Lodge Antarvedi directly through our website by filling the booking form, or call us at 096766 10537. We offer online booking with UPI payment.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in and check-out at Sri Krishna Lodge is flexible. It follows a 24-hour cycle — if you check in at 11:00 AM, you should vacate by 11:00 AM the next day.

Are pets allowed at Sri Krishna Lodge?

Yes, Sri Krishna Lodge Antarvedi is a pet-friendly lodge. Pets are welcome.

Is there a restaurant at lodges in Antarvedi?

Sri Krishna Lodge does not have a restaurant, but delicious meals are available at the Laxmi Narasimha Swamy Temple prasadam counter nearby, and there are several local restaurants within walking distance in Antarvedi.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation must be made at least 48 hours in advance for a full refund. During Antarvedi festival days and peak seasons, the cancellation policy may vary.

Is free parking available at hotels in Antarvedi?

Yes, Sri Krishna Lodge provides free parking for all guests. You can safely park your car or bike at our lodge.

How do I pay for room booking at Sri Krishna Lodge?

After booking, you'll see a QR code. Scan it with any UPI app (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm) and pay the amount. Our staff will confirm your booking after payment verification. We also accept cash at the lodge.

How to reach Antarvedi from Rajahmundry?

Antarvedi is approximately 100 km from Rajahmundry. You can take a bus from Rajahmundry to Amalapuram (60 km), then an auto or bus to Antarvedi (40 km). From Sakhinetipalli, Antarvedi is just 15 km. Private taxis and autos are also available from Amalapuram.

What are the nearby attractions around Antarvedi?

Antarvedi is famous for the Laxmi Narasimha Swamy Temple (Antarvedi Devasthanam), Antarvedi Beach (Godavari meets the Bay of Bengal), the ancient lighthouse, and the chariot (Ratham). It is a popular pilgrimage and tourist destination in East Godavari, Andhra Pradesh.

Is breakfast included with the room?

No, breakfast is not included. However, affordable meals and tiffin are available at nearby restaurants in Antarvedi.

Do you offer room service?

Yes, Sri Krishna Lodge offers limited room service from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
